You are implementing a SOUND SYSTEM for an existing web game called "Urinal Protocol".

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:

* This is a tension-based comedic puzzle game
* Sound must enhance:

  * hesitation
  * awkwardness
  * subtle humor
* DO NOT make it loud, cartoonish, or overwhelming

The goal is:
👉 Make the player *feel* uncomfortable in a funny way

---

# 1. CORE AUDIO PHILOSOPHY

1. Less sound = more impact
2. Silence is a feature, not a bug
3. Timing matters more than volume
4. Sounds should feel realistic and subtle

---

# 2. SOUND MANAGER ARCHITECTURE

Create:

soundManager.js

---

## REQUIRED API

play(eventName)
stop(eventName)
playAmbient()
setVolume(level)

---

## INTERNAL SYSTEM

* preload all sounds
* reuse audio instances (no re-creating)
* prevent overlapping spam
* implement priority system

---

# 3. SOUND PRIORITY SYSTEM (CRITICAL)

Only ONE high-priority sound can play at a time.

Priority order:

1. failure
2. selection
3. character reaction
4. ambient

If a higher priority sound plays:
→ lower ones must pause or stop

---

# 4. SOUND EVENT MAP

Define clearly:

---

## 4.1 HOVER

Trigger AFTER 150ms (hover intent)

* soft UI tick (very low volume)

DO NOT play repeatedly while hovering

---

## 4.2 DANGER HOVER

When hovering risky/invalid:

* very subtle tension tone
* low frequency hum

---

## 4.3 LOCK-IN (SELECTION)

Triggered on click:

* short muted “click”
* slightly deeper tone than hover

---

## 4.4 LOCK-IN SILENCE (IMPORTANT)

After click:

* NO SOUND for ~200–300ms

This creates tension before outcome.

---

## 4.5 FAILURE SOUND

Triggered after lock-in delay:

* short awkward sting
* slightly off-beat tone (not heroic)

Optional:

* very light “room tone dip” (ambient reduces briefly)

---

## 4.6 SUCCESS SOUND

* subtle confirmation tone
* soft, not celebratory

---

## 4.7 HOLD IT IN

* slight tension release tone
* small “relief” cue

---

## 4.8 PANTS INCIDENT (SPECIAL EVENT)

Sequence:

1. silence (300ms)
2. soft buildup tone
3. comedic but subtle “failure” cue

DO NOT make this slapstick.

---

Volume:

* very low (10–20%)

## DYNAMIC AMBIENT

* reduce ambient volume slightly during failure
* restore after

Use same timing constants as animation system:

hoverDelay = 150ms
lockDelay = 220ms
failTrigger = 300–500ms

---

# 8. SOUND DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

* avoid cartoon SFX
* avoid loud or sharp sounds
* avoid repetition fatigue
* keep everything subtle
