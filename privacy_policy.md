# Privacy Policy — Piss Protocol

**Last updated: April 3, 2026**

## Overview

Piss Protocol ("the Game", "we", "us") is a humour-based urinal etiquette puzzle game developed by the Piss Protocol team. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect any information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Player name**: When you choose to enter a player name for the leaderboard, we store this name alongside your score on our server.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Game scores**: High scores are stored on our server to power the public leaderboard. Scores are associated with the player name you provide.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

We do **not** collect:
- Real names, email addresses, or contact information
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Location data
- Health or biometric data
- Payment information
- Photos, microphone, or camera data
- Contacts or calendar data

## How We Use Your Information

- To display your score on the public in-game leaderboard
- To improve gameplay balance and difficulty

## Data Storage

Player names and scores are stored on our secure server hosted on Replit infrastructure. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

## Offline Data

The game caches high score data locally on your device (using the browser Cache API) so you can view scores when offline. This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

## Haptic Feedback

On Android devices, the game uses the device vibration motor for haptic feedback on correct/incorrect choices. No data is collected or transmitted as part of this feature.

## Children's Privacy

The game is rated for Everyone (with Crude Humor content descriptor). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information, contact us to have it removed.

## Data Retention

Leaderboard scores may be retained indefinitely. You may request deletion of your entry by contacting us.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at the app's Google Play Store listing.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this document.
